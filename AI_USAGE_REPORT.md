# AI Usage Report

## AI Tool Used

ChatGPT

## What I Asked AI To Do

1. Analyze the Edumerge Student Support & Ticket Management assignment and identify the required product workflows.
2. Help design the ticket lifecycle, including statuses, priorities, ownership, SLA, ageing, escalation, pending actions, and resolution tracking.
3. Generate and refine parts of the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototype.

## Prompt That Was Most Useful

"Design a practical student support and ticket management system for a college. The system should support ticket creation, statuses, priorities, staff assignment, SLA tracking, ageing, escalation, pending-action workflows, activity history, resolution tracking, and management visibility."

## Code Generated By AI

AI generated the initial HTML, CSS, and JavaScript structure for the prototype, including:

- Dashboard
- Ticket listing
- Ticket creation form
- Search and filtering
- Status and priority handling
- Staff assignment
- SLA and ageing calculations
- Activity history
- Browser persistence

## Code I Modified

I reviewed and tested the generated implementation and modified the prototype to improve:

- Pending-action workflow
- Pending reasons
- Resolution notes
- Resolution tracking
- Activity history
- Product workflow and validation

## AI Output That Was Wrong

During testing, some parts of the generated workflow did not initially provide enough explicit handling for pending actions and resolution tracking.

## How I Identified The Problem

I compared the implemented workflow against the assignment requirements and manually tested the ticket lifecycle from creation through pending and resolution.

## How I Fixed It

I added explicit pending reasons and resolution notes and tested the ticket lifecycle again to ensure the actions were reflected in the activity history.
